Privacy

Privacy policy

The short version: this site does not track you, sets no cookies, and runs no ads or analytics. If you send us a message, we use it to reply to you and for nothing else.

Effective September 17, 2026

Who we are

This site belongs to Stockwell Media Co, a studio based in Springfield, Missouri. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Stockwell Media Co. Questions about your information go to info@stockwellmedia.com or (816) 797-2739.

What we collect

What you send us. When you use the contact form we receive your name, email, and message, plus your business name, phone number, and the type of help you picked if you fill those in. When you email or call, we receive whatever you choose to share.

Spam protection. The contact form stores a one-way scrambled version of your IP address so it can limit repeat submissions. It cannot be turned back into your actual address.

Basic server logs. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub records technical details of each visit, such as IP address and browser type, for security and to keep the service running. We do not see or use those logs. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. There is no analytics software, no advertising pixel, no social media tracking, and no third-party scripts. Fonts and images are served from this site directly, so loading a page does not send your information to Google or anyone else. Because there is nothing to consent to, you will not see a cookie banner here.

If that ever changes, we will update this policy first and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

Do Not Track

Some browsers can send a "Do Not Track" signal. Because this site does not track visitors in the first place, there is nothing for that signal to switch off, and the site behaves the same either way. No third party is allowed to collect information about your browsing on this site or across other sites through it.

How we use it

  • To reply to your inquiry by email, phone, or text, using the contact details you gave us.
  • To prepare a proposal or do work you ask us to do.
  • To keep the contact form free of spam and abuse.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. We do not add you to a mailing list. We do not use it for advertising.

Text messages

If you give us your phone number, we may call or text you to answer your inquiry. That is a conversation, not a marketing list: we will not send you promotional texts unless you separately ask for them. If you ever want us to stop texting, reply STOP or just tell us, and we will.

Who helps us handle it

A few services carry your message from the form to us: Supabase stores contact form submissions, Cloudflare forwards email sent to our @stockwellmedia.com addresses, Google (Gmail) hosts our inbox, and Apple (iMessage) sends us a notification. They process the information only to provide those services. Beyond that, we share information only if the law requires it.

Where it is stored

Information you send us is stored and processed in the United States. This site is meant for people and businesses in the United States.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiries for as long as they are useful for the conversation or the work that follows. If we never end up working together, you can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us what we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email info@stockwellmedia.com and we will take care of it within 30 days. You never have to use the form: calling or emailing us directly works just as well.

We honor these requests for everyone, wherever you live, and we will never treat you differently for making one. If you live in a state with its own privacy law, such as California, the same email is how you exercise the rights that law gives you.

Client work

This policy covers this website. When we build dashboards, automations, or other systems that touch a client's business data, that data is handled under our agreement with that client and is used only to deliver the work.

Security

The site is served only over encrypted HTTPS. Contact form submissions are stored in a private database that the public cannot read, and access is limited to us. No method is perfect, but we take reasonable steps to protect what you send.

Children

This site is for businesses and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the date at the top.
