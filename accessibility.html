Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use this site, including people who use a screen reader, a keyboard instead of a mouse, or zoom to read. If something gets in your way, tell us and we will fix it.

Updated October 26, 2026

The standard we aim for

We aim for this site to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA. These are the guidelines most widely used to measure whether a website works for people with disabilities.

What we have done

  • Text and buttons have enough contrast to read, on both desktop and phone screens.
  • Every page can be used with a keyboard, and you can always see which link or field is selected.
  • Images that carry meaning have text descriptions, and decorative ones are hidden from screen readers.
  • Contact form fields are labeled, and errors are shown in words, not only in color.
  • Pages use real headings and declare their language, so screen readers can move through them and read them correctly.
  • Animations are short and do not flash.

We test every page against WCAG 2.2 AA with automated tools whenever the site changes. Automated tools cannot catch everything, so we also rely on you to tell us what they miss.

Tell us about a problem

If any part of this site is hard to use, email info@stockwellmedia.com or call (816) 797-2739. Let us know the page and what went wrong, and what device or assistive technology you were using if you know it. We will reply within two business days and work to fix the problem.

You never have to use the website to reach us. Anything you can do here, including asking about our services, you can do by email or phone instead.

Documents and client work

This statement covers stockwellmedia.com. If you need a proposal, agreement, or other document from us in a different format, ask and we will provide one. When we build websites and tools for clients, we build to the same WCAG 2.2 AA standard.
